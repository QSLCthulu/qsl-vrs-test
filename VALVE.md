## VRS Invitations and Seeding

- The Tournament Operator chooses the VRS list(s) and for each list, the number of Rosters to invite and the VRS ranks from which to start the invitations [S10].
- The Tournament Operator must invite a minimum of two Rosters from any single VRS list [S11].
- The Tournament Operator must use the most recent version of the VRS as of the Invite Date [S12].
- The Tournament Operator must invite Rosters sequentially, starting with the chosen starting VRS rank [S13].
- Directly invited VRS Rosters may only accept an invitation if: (a) at least three of the Participating Roster's Athletes were on the Invited Roster on the Invite Date and will play in each event match; (b) the Participating Roster satisfies the invite criteria (e.g., regional, demographic, etc.); and (c) the Roster has not previously declined an invitation to the same Tournament [S14].
- If any Rosters cannot accept or choose to decline the Direct VRS Invite, the Tournament Operator shall proceed down the VRS in order until all announced slots to the Tournament are filled [S15].
- In the event that there are fewer eligible VRS rosters than the number of announced Direct VRS Invites, the Tournament Operator may supplement with Open Qualifiers [S16].
- For every two Americas VRS invitations, the Tournament Operator may subdivide VRS invitations into one North America invitation and one South America invitation [S17].
- The Tournament Operator may subdivide Asia VRS invitations equally between Oceania, West Asia, and East Asia [S18].
- If the Tournament Operator subdivides a VRS region in this way, Rosters must have a plurality of citizenships from the relevant subregion (among the players from the region) [S19].
- If the Tournament Operator includes Wildcard Invites or Open Qualifiers, those Rosters must enter the Tournament at or before the first stage featuring a VRS invite [S20].
- A Directly Invited VRS Roster cannot start at a later stage than another Directly Invited VRS Roster with a higher VRS Rank [S21].
- Rosters without a Global VRS rank cannot be seeded higher than VRS Ranked Rosters [S22].
- Initial seeding will use the version of the VRS that was specified in the Additional Information announcement [S23].
- For every 8 Rosters invited from a VRS, 2 eligible Rosters ("Wildcard Invite") can be invited to the Tournament [S24].
- If the Tournament Operator chooses not to make use of previously announced Wildcard invitations, they may invite the next highest ranked Rosters from the VRS lists already announced for the Tournament [S25].
- A Roster shall only be eligible for a Wildcard Invite if it is ranked lower than 12 on the Global VRS list and fulfills at least one of the following requirements as of the Invitation Date: (a) includes at least 3 players who individually were members of a top 12 Roster on an Invite VRS List at any time during the preceding 6 months; or (b) has placed first or second place in any Tournament, of equal or lesser tier than the one issuing the invite, during the preceding 6 months [S26].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tournament as Tier 1, they must invite from one or more of the official VRS lists: Global, Americas, Europe, Asia [S27].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tier 1 Tournament, the Tournament must invite a minimum of 20 Rosters by Direct VRS Invite [S28].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tier 1 Tournament, the list of invited Rosters always starts with the Roster that has the top VRS rank (i.e., the #1 Roster) at the time of the Invite Date [S29].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tier 1 Tournament, the Main Stage must feature a minimum of 8 Rosters [S30].
- The Tournament Operator may additionally include Open Qualifiers for Tier 1 Tournaments [S31].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tournament as Tier 2, the Tournament Operator may invite entirely by one or more Open Qualifiers [S32].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tier 2 Tournament that includes any Direct VRS Invite, then the Tournament must invite a minimum of 4 Rosters by Direct VRS Invite [S33].
- Tournament Operator may apply additional filters to the VRS lists (e.g., "North America") insofar as they do not lead to the specific targeting of individual Teams or Rosters. For the avoidance of doubt, filtering by country or gender is permitted [S34].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tier 2 Tournament that includes any Direct VRS Invite, the VRS rank of the invited Rosters must be lower than 12 on the Global VRS at the time of the Invite Date [S35].
- For every three completed Tournaments, the Tournament Operator may operate one Wildcard Tournament of equivalent or lesser Tier [S36].
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tournament as a Wildcard Tournament, a Wildcard Tournament may feature an unlimited number of Wildcard invitations and has no minimum number of rosters participating in the Tournament [S37].
- Tournament Operator must specify the Tournaments associated with the Wildcard Tournament. Wildcard invitations must adhere to the same VRS list applied to the respective Tournaments. [S38]
- If the Tournament Operator defines a Tournament as a Wildcard Tournament, the Tournament Operator may additionally invite from the VRS or include Open Qualifiers [S39].

## Prize Pool and Payment

- The prize pool is 1500 USD and prize distribution is 1.9002. 4003. 1004. 100 [S45].
- Compensation for a given standing cannot exceed compensation for any higher standing (e.g., a qualifier cannot award a prize greater than any main event prize) [S41a].
- Compensation for a given standing must be equivalent to compensation for all equal standings (e.g., all prizes for identical placement in parallel matches must be identical) [S41b].

## Information Valve Requires Organizers to Publish

The Tournament Operator shall publish the following no later than the relevant deadlines:

- Tournament Dates and the identity of the Tournament Operator on the Announcement Date [S43].
- Tier 1 and Tier 1 Wildcard Tournaments: additional information no later than 10 months before the Tournament main event or two weeks prior to any Open Qualifier [S44a].
- Tier 2 Tournaments that feature VRS invitations and Tier 2 Wildcard Tournaments: additional information no later than two months before the Tournament main event or two weeks prior to any Open Qualifier [S44b].
- Tier 2 Tournament that does not feature any VRS invitations: additional information no later than two weeks before the Tournament main event or two weeks prior to any qualifier, with at least one week between announcement and registration [S44c].
- The Additional Information shall include: (a) a definition of the filters to the VRS (if any) that Direct VRS Invites will be based on; (b) the VRS rank from which invitations will begin; (c) the number of Rosters subject to a Direct VRS Invite and/or Wildcard Invite, if any; (d) the Invitation Date; (e) the VRS publication date from which Direct VRS Invites will be issued; (f) the VRS publication date which will be used for seeding; (g) the visa region where the Tournament takes place (e.g., USA, Schengen, China, etc.); (h) whether an Open Qualifier will be part of the Tournament and the rules for such an Open Qualifier; (i) which Tournament stages are online or LAN; (j) the prize pool and rules for its distribution; (k) to the degree that the Tournament Operator compensates Teams, Rosters or Athletes for appearing at Tournaments in any other way (e.g., guaranteed payments for appearances, revenue shares in sponsorships or media rights, etc.), comprehensive information of all such compensation; (l) any integrity or conduct rules that disqualify a Roster from a Direct VRS Invite (disqualification rules cannot be selectively applied at the discretion of the Tournament Operator; they must be transparent, based exclusively on specific objective criteria, and applied irrespective of the affected Roster or circumstances surrounding the Tournament) [S45].
- The disqualification rules (if any) must be published as part of Additional Information and the details of any disqualification decision must be published at the time it occurs [S52].
- Published information must be on a platform that does not allow modification of past posts (e.g., X.com), preserves version history (e.g., GitHub.com), or be preserved on an independent archiving platform (e.g., Internet Archive's Wayback Machine) [S46].
- If the event uses "first-come-first-served" registration, rosters' registration time and order must be publicly inspectable (e.g., Google form registration that posts to a view-only publicly visible sheet with timestamps) [S46].
- For Tier 1 Tournaments and Wildcard Tournaments starting between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2025: Announcement Date no later than 1 September 2024; Invite Date must follow the announcement of Additional Information by no fewer than 30 days [S42a].
- For Tier 1 Tournaments and Wildcard Tournaments starting between 1 January 2026 and 31 December 2026: Announcement Date no later than 1 January 2025; Invite Date must follow the announcement of Additional Information by no fewer than 60 days [S42b].
- For Tier 1 Tournaments and Wildcard Tournaments starting after 31 December 2026: Announcement Date at least 22 months prior to the start of the Tournament's main event; Invite Date must follow the announcement of Additional Information by no fewer than 6 months [S42c].
- For Tier 2 Tournaments that include Direct VRS Invites: Announcement Date at least 2 months before the start of the Tournament's main event; Invite Date must follow the announcement of Additional Information by no fewer than 30 days [S42d].
- For Tier 2 Tournaments that do not have any Direct VRS invites: Announcement Date at least two weeks before the start of the Tournament's main event [S42e].
